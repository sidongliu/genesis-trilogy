\section*{Introduction}\label{collection:intro}
How do a structured world, a physical interface and a subject capable of revising its own participation form together? HAFF, Q-RAIF and T-DOME retain these three responsibilities. The present volume follows their main sequence; Foundation, Consciousness, Imprint, Brain Anchor and the Q-RAIF companions develop its additional mathematical and explanatory interfaces.

The world line now puts physical selection before arbitrary redescription. Interactions select persistent blocks, coherence-decay rates give a coupling-dependent Euclidean relation, and conditions for a spatial interpretation become an explicit target. Transfer dynamics are not derived from dephasing; response-based distances require a separately identified reversible generator. Records can recover an unknown event order in a specified protocol, while time parametrization remains a different question. The gravitational line uses common-source and modular consistency conditions before turning to finite sourced-response constructions.

The interface line establishes conditional relationships among propagation, operator representation and the cost of control; it does not identify positive and Lorentzian metrics by name. The subject line gives task-information prices, a finite history-formation theorem and continued inquiry and repair from an acquired model. Those advances reduce specific supplied inputs without pretending that every device or every law has been generated.

Finite A has a precise meaning: one semiclassical mean-action scalar/JT history carries an acquired record into a physical source, geometric response and later X-basis reading, with a signal of order $10^{-9}$. Sustained fixed-capacity thermal updating B and joint sharp heat export with effective return C retain stronger conjectural status. Neither establishes universal explanatory closure. The Imprint Chain's typed interface table and the postscript's cosmic formation-and-return conjecture keep the larger programme visible without borrowing those conjectures as proofs.

\section*{Reading map}
\begin{description}
\item[HAFF-A] Physical coarse-graining selects persistent distinctions, with a coupling-dependent coherence-decay relation. Transfer responses and a spatial dictionary require further inputs; finite geometry certificates test this generative route.
\item[HAFF-B] Interaction-selected blocks, protected content and finite lifetimes establish which relations survive. Stable sectors are not automatically tensor factors or spatial sites.
\item[HAFF-C] Acquired history changes the interventions available to a body. The relation between functional agency and experience remains a separate explanatory question.
\item[HAFF-D] One conserved physical source enters modular and geometric response constraints. Conditional field-law synthesis, finite physical return and stronger thermal conjectures have distinct assumptions.
\item[HAFF-E] Actual instruments connect permissions to stable and public records. Public agreement requires more than redundancy to certify correctness.
\item[HAFF-F] Endpoint records identify an unknown exchange-event order under specified gates. Ordinary copying can fail this test; acquisition, renewal and time parametrization remain distinct.
\item[HAFF-G] Internal programs alter permissions and sources. Protocol-relative indistinguishability constrains complete explanation without creating a universal final boundary.
\item[HAFF-PS] Cosmic origin and closure return as a formation-and-renewal conjecture, alongside conditional finite-body participation bounds.
\item[QR-A] Local propagation and the single quadratic shell constrain Clifford and Lorentzian readout. A common cone still needs the stated speed-alignment dynamics.
\item[QR-B] A centred physical coupling gives a state-independent cost criterion and a conditional bridge to the propagation form. Alignment is an additional physical question.
\item[QR-C] Discarded control ports and closed propagation give different exact representation results. Finite source, clock and renewal witnesses test their physical implementation.
\item[TD-I] Task-relevant retained distinctions have usable work value. Compression prices a current feedback posterior, not every future learning task.
\item[TD-II] Finite paid training selects a two-bit reading frame that beats a history-independent direction at one test read, even with a free coefficient oracle. Total resources are not matched; the independent-replay control tests history relevance, not a separation from general learning.
\item[TD-III] Drift detection, inquiry and paid repair use different functions of the same acquired belief. Terminal compression and continuing self-revision need not retain the same information.
\end{description}
