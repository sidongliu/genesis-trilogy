## Description

This paper is a companion paper to Q-RAIF A. It does not attempt to derive special relativity, four-momentum, or particle mass generation from first principles. Instead, it establishes a conditional recovery theorem: given the prior Q-RAIF A result that admissible finite read-out from $(\mathcal{A}_U,\Omega)$ induces an effective Lorentzian geometry, the standard mass-energy relation is recovered as a downstream theorem once admissible four-momentum is defined on the effective read-out sector through a local Stone-generator construction.

The contribution is therefore a compatibility result rather than a standalone derivation. It shows that the Q-RAIF A Lorentzian emergence framework preserves the ordinary relativistic mass-shell relation and the rest-frame formula $E=mc^2$ without reintroducing them as independent postulates. The theorem-level claim is intentionally narrow. The paper does not derive the Standard Model mass spectrum, the Higgs mechanism, QCD confinement, or the numerical value of $c$.

Within the larger Genesis Trilogy architecture, this deposit should be read as a technical Q-RAIF companion paper that clarifies how standard relativistic mass-energy structure is recovered inside the Q-RAIF A emergence setting.
