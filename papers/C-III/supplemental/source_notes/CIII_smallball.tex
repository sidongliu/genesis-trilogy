\section{The exact small-ball moment in one dimension}\label{app:chm}

In $d=1$ the CHM modular Hamiltonian of an interval of radius $R$ is $K_B=\frac{\pi}{R}\int_{-R}^{R}(R^2-x^2)T_{00}(x)\,dx$~\cite{CasiniHuertaMyers2011}. Expanding $T_{00}$ about the centre, the coefficient of the $2k$-th derivative, made dimensionless by $R^{-(2k+2)}$, is
\[
c_k=\frac{\pi}{R}\cdot\frac{1}{(2k)!}\int_{-R}^{R}(R^2-x^2)x^{2k}\,dx\cdot R^{-(2k+2)}=\frac{4\pi}{(2k+1)(2k+3)(2k)!},
\]
using $\int_{-R}^{R}(R^2-x^2)x^{2k}dx=4R^{2k+3}/((2k+1)(2k+3))$. The factorial suppression is a property of the signal in this normalization. It does not by itself give a fluctuation kernel, a normalized Fisher information or a permitted measurement, so it does not instantiate Proposition~\ref{prop:horizon}. Consciousness~II turns the higher-dimensional remainder into an exact multipole series with normalized directions and, under a supplied readout noise, a conditional Fisher information with $\log I_k=-4k\log k+O(k)$, which instantiates Proposition~\ref{prop:horizon} with $\alpha=4$~\cite{PaperII}; a physical readout noise is still to be derived.
